Cabify's mission and culture resonate strongly with me. I appreciate your focus on improving mobility in cities, an area that has a real and positive impact on people's daily lives. Your principles and values feel genuinely lived, not just stated, and I particularly value the emphasis on coherence, accountability, and transparency.

I also find it remarkable that Cabify publishes its handbook openly. This level of openness reflects maturity as an organization and gives candidates a clear understanding of how the company works and what it stands for. It is refreshing to see a company that aligns its internal culture with the way it communicates externally.

Overall, Cabify strikes me as a place where thoughtful engineering, purpose-driven work, and continuous learning come together. This is the kind of environment in which I thrive and would be excited to contribute.
